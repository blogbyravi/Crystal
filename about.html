NATURE. TRADITION. PERSONAL CONNECTION.

A thoughtful approach to your personal journey.

At Devine Crystal & Astrology Services, we bring together the beauty of natural crystals and the traditional practices of astrology and numerology. Whether you are choosing your first crystal, finding a meaningful gift or exploring a new perspective on yourself, we welcome your curiosity.

Our approach starts with a conversation. We take time to understand what you are looking for, explain your options in simple language and help you choose a crystal or consultation that feels right for you, without pressure or promises of a particular outcome.

WHAT WE OFFER

Three ways to explore.

01

Natural crystals

Discover amethyst, rose quartz and clear quartz, chosen for their natural colours, textures and individual character. We help you find pieces for your home, a thoughtful gift or your own collection.

Ask us about choosing and caring for your crystal, or about its traditional symbolism. Every stone is unique, and we can discuss available pieces before you order.

02

Astrology consultations

Explore the traditional interpretation of a birth chart through a personal consultation. Astrology uses your birth date, time and place to discuss themes associated with personality, relationships and life transitions.

We offer a space to ask questions and reflect on those themes in the context of your own experiences. One astrology consultation costs ₹501. Share your question to discuss availability and the details needed for a session.

Request a consultation · ₹501 ↗
03

Numerology guidance

Numerology is a traditional practice that assigns symbolic meanings to numbers associated with your name and date of birth. A session can explore concepts such as life path and name numbers as prompts for self-reflection.

We explain these interpretations in an approachable way, helping you consider personal strengths, interests and recurring themes. Contact us to learn about session options, pricing and what to prepare.

Enquire about numerology ↗

Our consultations are for spiritual exploration and personal reflection. Astrology and numerology are not scientifically validated methods of predicting events, and crystals are not medical treatments. These services do not replace professional medical, legal or financial advice.
